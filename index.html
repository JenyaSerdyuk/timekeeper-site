What it is

Time Keeper is an object at the intersection of philosophy, hardware and art. From the moment you start it, it fixes that moment on the blockchain and counts every second after it. The moment can never be changed, by anyone. It is bound to you and your family line forever.

Why Time Keeper exists

We invested our own money, time, soul, depression and nerves to release this product, because it is beautiful and it has to exist. This project can be copied, but no one can put as much soul into it as we did.

We made it because people spend their time as if it were endless, because it would be good to leave something behind for hundreds of years, for your future generations, and because we wanted one for ourselves. We believe there are others just like us.

Ways to use it

Your time keeps disappearing somewhere. This is the keeper of your time, and it has an existential meaning as the main value in this world. There is nothing more valuable than your time. With Time Keeper, your time will never disappear. Below are the ways to use it that we know, but you can come up with your own. The main thing is that it inspires you.

Pass it down as a family relic

What will you leave to your family tree for hundreds of years ahead? It will never be like it used to be. Now your relatives will know about you even thousands of years from now. Even 300 years from now, your heirs will get a hello from an unusual ancestor. Time Keeper is made to be handed down, as something that will remain for hundreds of years after you. The infinite family album feature will let them see all the ancestors thanks to whom they were born, even hundreds of years later, and learn more about them

Use it as a deep philosophical gift

If you don’t know what to give someone who has everything, give them time. For someone close to you, as a reminder of how important life is. You do not give them a thing, you give them a moment that will always remind them of you. Their count begins the second they start it and stays theirs for life

Remember time is short

Your seconds are too important to take for granted. The countdown from 2.5 billion shows how much of an average life may be left. Seeing it every day changes how you spend your time. A reason to stop and think, an object for self-reflection and self-improvement

Mark an important start

Use it as a symbolic reminder of the most important event in your life, one that will stay with you until the end. The birth of a child, your own wedding or a friends’ wedding, the start of a business or a new stage of life. These are turning points that can be marked with a symbolic gift

Quit a bad habit

Use it as support that helps you find the strength not to give up in a hard fight. Start it on the first day without a destructive habit, so you never go back to it. After a few million seconds nobody wants to start from zero

A reason for anxiety or a motivation to live?

While we were creating it, many people told us this product might make them anxious. Technically, yes. But why doesn’t a careless attitude towards the most valuable and the only irreplaceable resource in life cause the same anxiety?

What if you lose it?

Everything is saved on the blockchain. You will be able to get access to your time again after paying a fine, because time must be valued

A legacy that grows in value

Time Keeper is made to outlive its first owner. The moment it holds is fixed on the blockchain and the count never stops, so the object means more with every year it exists. Passed to children and grandchildren, it carries the exact second it was started and the life line collected over decades: photos, voice notes and videos.

As a personal art object it works like a painting signed by time itself. The first 1,000 is the most valuable of all collections: it will help us grow and expand, and its owners will hold the most valuable collection, because in the future their art objects will have the most seconds of all. The longer it counts, the more history it holds.

Your life line

Once a month, you upload 3 photos, 1 video and a short note, like a small letter to yourself. An eternal diary, every month, to build the album of your life that your relatives can look through hundreds of years from now. Today we cannot see photos, let alone the whole story, even of our closest relatives, not to mention those who lived hundreds of years before us. In the future this problem will not exist.

You, the way you lived, what you went through, will stay forever. People who do not exist yet and will only be born hundreds of years from now will be able to learn a lot about you and take care of this family line. Register, post 3 photos and 1 video once a month, and keep the album of your life.
