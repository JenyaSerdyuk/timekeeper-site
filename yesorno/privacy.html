Privacy and rules

Updated November 28, 2026 · WHY TF NOT STUDIO

Short version: we keep only what the app needs to work. We never sell your data. You can delete your account and everything you made at any time in Settings

What we keep

  • An account ID. If you use Sign in with Apple, your first name if you share it
  • Your country and app language, so questions come to you in your language
  • Questions, photos, votes, comments and reactions you post
  • Reports and blocks you make, to keep the feed safe

What others see

Your questions, photos, comments and your user name are public in the app. Anonymous questions hide your name. Your votes are private: others only see the total

Checks and translation

Before a question goes live, its text and photos are checked by AI (Anthropic) for content that breaks the rules, and translated into other languages. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models

Simulated votes

While the app is new, part of the votes on each question are simulated. They always follow the real votes and never change the answer

Where data is stored

On secure servers of Supabase. We use no ads and no tracking

Rules

No nudity, violence, hate, harassment, drugs, spam, or other people's private data. Questions that break the rules are removed. Three reports hide a question, and authors who break the rules again lose access

Delete your data

Settings → Delete account removes your account, questions, photos, votes and comments for good
